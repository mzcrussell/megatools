About QuickTools Hub

QuickTools Hub is a collection of practical online utilities designed to make everyday digital tasks easier. Use the QR code studio to share links and preview your QR code live on T-shirts, caps and coffee mugs, extract text from images in 50+ languages, convert PDFs to text or text to PDF, generate UUIDs, resize and convert images, format JSON, calculate percentages, age, GPA, VAT and loan installments, and test typing speed.

Our goal is to keep the tools straightforward, accessible and easy to use on desktop and mobile devices. Many operations run directly in your browser, so the information you enter stays private on your device.

How to use QuickTools Hub

  1. Choose a tool from the tool cards or search for it by name.
  2. Enter the information or select the file requested by that tool.
  3. Select the action button to generate, extract, convert, scan or process your result.
  4. Copy or download your result when the tool provides that option.

For the QR scanner, allow camera permission when prompted. For OCR, PDF and image processing, select a file from your device. Always check important results before sharing or publishing them.
