Tips for Creating Scannable Barcodes

  • Select the correct barcode format for your application.
  • Enter valid characters and numbers.
  • Keep sufficient blank space around the barcode.
  • Use strong contrast between the bars and background.
  • Avoid stretching or distorting the barcode image.
  • Test printed labels using a compatible barcode scanner.
Retail product identification: Generating a barcode image does not register a product number or provide an official GS1 identifier. Obtain valid product identifiers and follow applicable retailer requirements before commercial use.

What is an SVG barcode?

SVG stands for Scalable Vector Graphics. It allows barcode images to be resized without the pixelation associated with raster images.

Can I use these barcodes for retail products?

You can generate barcode graphics using valid product identifiers. Retail registration, GS1 standards and marketplace requirements may apply.

What is the difference between EAN-13 and UPC-A?

EAN-13 uses 13 digits and is widely used internationally. UPC-A uses 12 digits and is commonly used in North American retail.

Which barcode format is suitable for inventory?

Code 128 and Code 39 are commonly used for internal inventory systems. Choose the format supported by your scanner and inventory software.
