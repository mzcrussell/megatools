How to Use the Free PDF Toolkit

How to merge PDF files

  1. Open the PDF Toolkit.
  2. Select the PDF files you want to combine.
  3. Choose Merge selected PDFs.
  4. Click Process PDF and save the result.

How to extract pages from a PDF

  1. Select your PDF document.
  2. Choose Extract pages from first PDF.
  3. Enter a page range such as 1-3,5.
  4. Process and download the extracted pages.

How to rotate PDF pages

  1. Select your PDF document.
  2. Choose Rotate all pages in first PDF.
  3. Select your preferred rotation angle.
  4. Process and download your updated PDF.
