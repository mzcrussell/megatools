Frequently asked questions

Is this a guarantee of security?

No. It is an educational estimate and cannot detect every predictable password pattern.

Is my password uploaded?

No, checking is performed locally.

What is a good password length?

A unique password or passphrase of 12–16 or more characters is a useful starting point.
