Fill/Crop vs. Fit/Padding

Fill / Crop

Fills the entire target area while maintaining the image's proportions. Some parts of the original image may be cropped.

Fit / Padding

Preserves the complete original image within the target dimensions. Extra space may appear around the image as padding.

Tips for Better Social Media Images

  • Use clear, high-resolution source images.
  • Keep important text and logos within safe margins.
  • Avoid stretching images out of proportion.
  • Use Fit/Padding when the entire image must remain visible.
  • Use Fill/Crop when an edge-to-edge design is preferred.
  • Review the final image before publishing.

Resizing changes image dimensions but cannot restore missing details in a low-resolution photograph.
