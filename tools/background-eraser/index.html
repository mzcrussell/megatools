Remove a plain, single-color background from an image in your browser. Adjust color tolerance and download a transparent PNG.

Frequently asked questions

Does it remove any background?

It works best with a nearly uniform background; complex scenes and hair edges need AI segmentation.

Is my image uploaded?

The processing is performed locally in your browser.

What format is exported?

A transparent PNG image.
