What Should a Professional Resume Include?

1. Contact Information

Include your full name, professional email address and phone number. Add your city or professional profile link when relevant.

2. Professional Summary

Write a short introduction explaining your relevant experience, strengths and career goals. Tailor this section to the position you are applying for.

3. Work Experience

List relevant positions, employers, employment dates and achievements. Start with your most recent experience.

4. Education

Include relevant degrees, institutions and graduation dates. Students may also highlight relevant coursework or academic achievements.

5. Skills

List relevant technical, professional and communication skills. Only include skills that you can demonstrate honestly.

Tips for Writing a Better Resume

  • Customize your resume for each job application.
  • Use clear headings and consistent formatting.
  • Keep your professional summary concise.
  • Highlight measurable achievements where possible.
  • Use relevant keywords from the job description naturally.
  • Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
  • Check spelling and grammar before submitting.
  • Use a professional filename, such as Alex-Morgan-Resume.pdf.

Resume vs. CV: What Is the Difference?

A resume is generally a concise document highlighting relevant skills, experience and qualifications for a particular job.

A curriculum vitae, commonly called a CV, may contain more detailed academic and professional information, particularly for research and academic positions.

In many countries, the terms resume and CV are used interchangeably for ordinary job applications. Always follow the employer's instructions.

How to Make Your Resume ATS-Friendly

Many employers use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to organize and review job applications.

  • Use standard headings such as Experience, Education and Skills.
  • Include relevant job-specific keywords.
  • Avoid unnecessary graphics and complicated formatting.
  • Use readable fonts and consistent spacing.
  • Submit the file format requested by the employer.

A clean resume may improve readability, but no resume builder can guarantee compatibility with every ATS or guarantee a job interview.

How to Save Your Resume as PDF

  1. Complete your resume information.
  2. Review the generated preview.

PDF documents are commonly used for sharing resumes because they help preserve document formatting. Follow the employer's requested file format when applying.
